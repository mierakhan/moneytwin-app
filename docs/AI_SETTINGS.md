# AI settings

- Model: deepseek · deepseek-flash. One model for every AI feature.
- Whose key pays: Your key (you bill the customer).
- Called from: Your server, keys in .env.
- Keys live only in .env on the server.
- AI budget per customer: RM20.00 a month.

## What each customer’s account controls
- A monthly AI budget per customer, with an alert at 80%
- AI usage and cost per customer on a dashboard
- Prompt versions, with roll-back
- Change the model or provider in settings, not in code
- Remove personal data before a model call
- Customers can turn AI features off

## AI features and their models
- AI Expense Analysis (summarise): deepseek-flash, about 30 uses per customer a month
- Financial Simulation (summarise): deepseek-flash, about 40 uses per customer a month
- AI Finance Chatbot (chat with the user): deepseek-flash, about 100 uses per customer a month
